    From render settings
    to the final look, in one tab.

    RenderFlow Studios is a Blender add-on built for video production. It gathers the whole flow — pick render settings → set up the camera → render → finish — into one sidebar tab and a layer-based compositor.

    Overview

    RenderFlow Studios turns "settings → shooting → finishing" in Blender into one continuous flow. Switch a complete set of render settings in one click — from 80s / 90s retro to cinematic — frame your shot with the camera and safe areas in the sidebar, then finish the render by stacking layers. No hand-built node trees required.

    Features

    Render Style All the settings in one click

    Engine, resolution, samples, fps and color management switch together, from retro to cinematic. → Render Style

    My Presets Keep your own settings

    Save tuned settings under a name and pick them from the same grid as the built-in styles. → My Presets

    Camera Camera control for video

    Pick the camera, set the lens and depth of field, and switch the projection — all from the sidebar. → Camera

    Guides Safe areas per camera

    Safe areas and layout guides are stored per camera, so a cinemascope camera and a vertical-shorts camera keep their own. → Guides (Safe Area)

    Compositor Layers Finish by stacking layers

    Drag to reorder, opacity, blend modes, groups and before / after comparison — built on Blender's own nodes. → Compositor Layers

    FX & Looks 22 FX filters and 9 Looks

    Optical effects like blur, glow and chromatic aberration; watercolor, oil and line art; 8-bit / 16-bit; cinematic grades. → FX Filters / Looks

    One shot in 4 steps

    1StylePick a render style and apply it. Engine to color, set at once. 2CameraSet the lens and depth of field; frame the shot with safe areas. 3RenderAs usual: F12 for a still, Ctrl+F12 for an animation. 4FinishGrade with compositor layers and add texture with FX and Looks.

    Requirements

    Item Details
    Blender 4.5 LTS (recommended). Works on 4.0 and later
    OS Windows 10 / 11, macOS 12 or later, Linux (as supported by Blender)
    Render engine EEVEE / Cycles (the cinematic preset uses 512 Cycles samples — GPU rendering recommended)
    License activation Internet is needed only to request the key and activate once (works offline afterwards)

    See Requirements for details.
